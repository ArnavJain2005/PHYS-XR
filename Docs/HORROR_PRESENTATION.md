## The game and gallery

*The Presence* is a short first-person horror game designed as a run of under five minutes, with one location and one antagonist. You arrive at Blackthorn House three weeks after its caretaker vanished. The power is out, and something tall stands at the end of the corridor.

1. **Find the fuse** on the kitchen table. The first time you see The Hollow clearly, it lunges once. The lunge is telegraphed and can be interrupted.
2. **Restore the power** in the utility room. The surge drives the ghost back, and a checkpoint is saved.
3. **Get out.** Leaving the utility room commits the encounter: The Hollow appears between you and the front door, warns, and attacks if you come within reach.
4. **Your only tool** is a candle lantern. Raise it at the right moment and the ghost staggers and retreats. Miss, and you wait for it to recharge.
5. **Escape** to the ending, where a recording plays and a figure watches from the upper window.

The Hollow is a *character* before it is a system. It trails you at a distance, stops and stares when something is wrong, searches the house when it hears a knock in the real room, recoils from light, and lunges only after a visible warning.

## Direction

A storm-bound Victorian house on the Blackthorn coast, its caretaker missing, the power out. The house is kept; the work deepens it rather than replacing it.

- **Two temperatures of light.** Cold blue-black night through the windows and in the shadows; warm tungsten in the few practical lights. The red emergency lamps mark rooms without flooding them. The player's lantern is the warmest, closest light.
- **Darkness hides, it does not erase.** A trilight ambient and a small lift keep floors, door frames and silhouettes readable, so the player can navigate and can *see something standing in a doorway*.
- **A figure that is wrong at every distance.** At range, a too-tall hooded silhouette with arms that reach its knees. Closer, a bloodless skull face with a jaw hanging too far open, ribs through a torn shroud, overlong fingers.
- **Restraint.** No permanent heavy grain, fringing or red wash; effects arrive with the ghost and leave with it. The score withdraws to near silence on its own.

## The Hollow

**Model.** Seven parts modelled in Blender 5.2 by script ([`Tools/blender/build_hollow.py`](../Tools/blender/build_hollow.py), source file `Tools/blender/Hollow.blend`), exported as `Assets/PHYSXR/Resources/PHYSXR/Hollow/Hollow.fbx`:

| Part | Hangs from | Triangles |
|---|---|---|
| Head (skull face, deep sockets, unhinged jaw, nasal cavity) | `Head` | 2,600 |
| Hood (tattered cowl, open at the face) | `Head` | 2,200 |
| Neck (stretched, vertebrae and sinew) | `Neck` | 1,100 |
| Torso (wasted ribcage, spine, shoulder blades) | `Chest` | 3,200 |
| Robe (torn burial shroud: strips, holes, chest gash, arm openings) | `Body` | 5,200 |
| Upper arm, forearm with hand and five overlong fingers (×2, mirrored) | `Arm`, `Fore` | 900 + 2,200 |

About 20,300 triangles for the whole figure. Organic parts are metaball fields (positive and negative ellipsoids) converted to meshes, given a displacement relief, decimated and UV-unwrapped; cloth parts are built with bmesh from a seeded generator and solidified. Import settings are fixed in code by `Assets/PHYSXR/Editor/HollowModelImport.cs` (meshes only, readable for the cloth, no materials or rig).

Each part is authored in the local space of the transform it hangs from, so `HollowVisual`'s existing procedural animation (per-behaviour poses, glitches, walk bob) drives the new body unchanged. If the meshes are missing, the original primitive figure is built instead.

**Materials** (`HollowAssets`): bloodless, mottled, veined skin with a clammy sheen and a very faint cold self-light, so the face, ribs and hands keep a pallor in near darkness without glowing; dark, damp grave linen with a woven normal map for the shroud and cowl (double-sided); two pinpoint emissive eyes set deep in the sockets. Textures are generated by `ProceduralTextures` (`GhostSkin`, `GraveLinen`, `GraveLinenNormal`).

**Motion** (`HollowVisual`, driven by `GhostMotionController`):

- *The head leads.* The head turns to the player well before the body, whose idle turning was slowed from 240°/s to 110°/s (the attack keeps the agent's fast turning, so strike timing is unchanged). While retreating, the head stays on the player as the body moves away. While searching (INVESTIGATE travel and linger) and while staggered, it does not look at the player.
- *Total stillness.* Every 7-16 s, if it is standing still in an idle pose, every idle motion stops for 1.2-3 s; it ends with a quick re-orientation of the head. Any movement or pose change breaks it at once.
- *Breathing* lifts the ribs; *cloth*: the robe's vertices sway with two travelling waves weighted by distance below the collar and trail when it moves (CPU, skipped when the robe is off screen).
- The arms splay slightly more so the hands clear the robe.
- Seeded timing: a replay looks the same.

**Sound**: a low presence (sub tones with audible harmonics, wide spread, 1-9 m) whenever it is manifested, and cloth dragging over boards that follows its ground speed. Every state's sound is in [Ghost audio by state](#ghost-audio-by-state).

## The attack

The gameplay timing is unchanged: WINDUP 1.2 s (the lantern's window to interrupt), STRIKE 0.3 s (hits within 1.6 m and 60°), RECOVER 2.0 s, cooldown 6 s. What changed is how those seconds are played (`GhostMotionController`, `HollowVisual`):

| Beat | When | Body | Sound | Music |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coil | WINDUP start until 0.36-0.54 s before its end | Crouches, arms drawn back, head locked on the player, eyes dimmed; creeps at 0.8 m/s; idle sway and glitches stop | one quiet intake of breath (`GhostInhale`) | drops to near silence in about 0.15 s |
| Lunge | the rest of the WINDUP | Thrown forward and stretched, arms out with one leading, eyes flaring; bursts to 6.5 m/s (acceleration 40 m/s² instead of 12) | **the scream** (`GhostScream`), once, on the burst's first frame, on its own high-priority voice | silent; the scream lands alone |
| Strike | STRIKE, 0.3 s | Arms close in a clawing reach, leading arm further | whoosh (`StrikeWhiff`) on entry | silent |
| Hit | the frame the strike connects (`StrikeHit`) | stops | impact (`StrikeImpact`), only here | — |
| Miss | STRIKE ends without a hit | — | nothing more: the whoosh was the attack | — |
| Recover | RECOVER, 2.0 s | hunched, head down | breath only | returns over about 2 s, held below its peak by the release rule |

Not every attack is the same: the burst length cycles through 0.46, 0.36 and 0.54 s, the leading arm and head tilt swap sides on alternate attacks, and the scream cycles through three variations (pitch contour and length) with a small pitch offset, all deterministic so replays match.

Cancelled attacks: the lantern can interrupt in the coil or the lunge (never in the strike). Interrupted in the coil, there is no scream, whoosh or impact; interrupted after the scream started, the scream is faded out in about 0.1 s. Either way the ghost cries out in pain (`GhostRecoil`) and staggers. If the strike kills, the scream is left to finish under the death sting; if the ghost is put away (`Halt(hide)`), everything it is saying stops.

The movement stays on the NavMesh agent with the same stopping distance (0.9 m), so the lunge cannot pass through walls, furniture or the player, and nothing teleports. The faster burst means a player who backs away during the coil gains ground early and loses it in the lunge; the strike's range and angle are unchanged.

The first-sighting lunge (E5) that lands before the encounter is committed is a feint: the strike connects, so it plays the impact, but the game does not kill (it plays a softer death sting). The ghost has no health and cannot be damaged or killed, so there is no damage or death vocalisation for it; the lantern recoil is its "hurt" reaction.

## Ghost audio by state

Every sound the ghost makes goes through `GhostMotionController` and raises `SoundPlayed`, which the encounter self-test records (checks A01-A05). Silence is a choice in several rows.

| State or event | Sound | Trigger | Spatial | Repetition | Stop / fade | Validated by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dormant (not in the house) | silence | — | — | — | all voices stopped (`SetDormant`) | T20 (full restart: dormant) |
| Manifest / appear | `GhostManifest` | first sighting, encounter, restarts | 3D, 2-30 m | per manifestation | one-shot | capture tour |
| Disappear (flicker out, put away) | `GhostVanish` | `Manifest(flicker)` while visible, `Halt(hide)` | 3D, 2-30 m | per disappearance | one-shot; the loops stop on `Halt(hide)` | not checked automatically |
| Presence, any manifested state | `GhostPresence` loop | manifested | 3D, 1-9 m, spread 120° (direction deliberately vague) | continuous | follows visibility; stops when dormant/hidden | capture tour |
| Idle, watching, following | `GhostBreath` loop | manifested | 3D, 1-10 m | continuous | stops when dormant/hidden | capture tour |
| Moving (trail, stalk, investigate, retreat, lunge) | `GhostDrag` loop | ground speed | 3D, 1-11 m | continuous | volume follows speed (0 at rest), fades 1.5/s | capture tour |
| Turning | silence | — | — | — | — | — |
| Investigate: setting off | `GhostThud` | INVESTIGATE starts (often a physical disturbance) | 3D, 2-30 m | per behaviour start | one-shot | A05 (no scream while investigating) |
| Investigate: searching | `GhostWhisper` loop | arrived and lingering | 3D, 1-8 m | continuous | fades in 0.6/s, out 1.5/s when it leaves or anything else starts | not checked automatically |
| Warn | `GhostScreech` | WARN starts within 14 m | 3D, 2-30 m | at most every 6 s | one-shot | S09 (telegraph) |
| Warn: escalation | `GhostScreech` (0.8) + light flicker | a running WARN becomes the VERY_NEAR one (X2) | 3D, 2-30 m | at most every 2 s | one-shot | Q20 (responsiveness self-test) |
| Protect | `ProtectHum` loop | PROTECT active | 3D, 1.5-12 m | continuous | stops when PROTECT ends | — |
| Attack: coil | `GhostInhale` | WINDUP entry | 3D, 2-30 m | per attack | one-shot | A01-A04 (per-attack counts) |
| Attack: lunge | `GhostScream` (3 variations) | first frame of the burst | 3D, 2.5-35 m, priority 0 | exactly once per attack | faded out in 0.1 s if the attack is cancelled; cut when hidden | A01 (once, inside the burst), A02 (none if cancelled before), A03 (cut if cancelled after), A04, A05 (only in windups) |
| Attack: strike | `StrikeWhiff` (whoosh) | STRIKE entry | 3D, 2-30 m | once per strike | one-shot | A01, A03 (none if cancelled), A04 |
| Confirmed hit | `StrikeImpact` | `StrikeHit` (strike connected) | 3D, 2-30 m | once per hit | one-shot | A04, A05 (impacts = hits) |
| Missed strike | silence after the whoosh | STRIKE ends without a hit | — | — | — | A05 (no impact without a hit) |
| Lantern hit (stagger, its "hurt") | `GhostRecoil` (2 variations) | `TryInterrupt` succeeds | 3D, 2-30 m | per interruption | one-shot | A02 |
| Retreat | `GhostHiss` | RETREAT starts | 3D, 2-30 m | per behaviour start | one-shot | — |
| Player death / feint | the game's `DeathSting` (2D), not the ghost's | `GameFlowController` | 2D | — | — | T17a, S04c |
| Ghost death or defeat | not implemented (the ghost cannot die) | — | — | — | — | — |

Restarts: every ghost voice is a child of the ghost and is destroyed with the scene, so nothing outlives a restart (T18e: the number of `AudioSource`s does not grow over ten restarts).

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| Window light: each window's lightning spot now keeps a dim cold moon spill between strikes; three (entry hall SW, kitchen N, dining E) cast soft shadows, so a strike throws the frame across the floor. ## Colour grade and post-processing

`HorrorPostFx` builds the profile at runtime (no asset to drift):

- ACES; contrast 15, saturation −18; shadows pushed cool and highlights warm (Shadows/Midtones/Highlights); a very small lift (+0.004) so blacks hold detail. (A first pass at +0.018 turned the dark hall into a milky blue haze in the capture review and was pulled back.)
- At rest: vignette 0.30, thin grain 0.22, chromatic aberration 0.02 (was 0.36 / 0.30 / 0.08).
- With the ghost near (0 at 9 m → 1 at 1.5 m): vignette, grain and fringing rise, colour drains further.
- During WINDUP/STRIKE: the red bleed (unchanged) plus a slight inward lens pull.
- Death: unchanged.

## Sound and score

**Adaptive score** (`TensionScore`, three procedural layers, all original). States: exploration (the drone bed, which withdraws to near silence for 14-24 s every 50-90 s when nothing is happening), tension (strings as the ghost is seen closing), investigation (strings held at "unease" while the ghost searches, even out of sight), imminent danger (pulse when it is near or warning), attack (the music drops out, see [The attack](#the-attack)), post-encounter (it cannot climb back past unease for 9 s) and recovery (a slow fall, 0.12/s, never a loop reset):

| Layer | Cue | Audible when |
|---|---|---|
| Drone | `ScoreDrone`: low A / B♭ / E cluster with independent swells and filtered air | from the start (bed) |
| Strings | `ScoreStrings`: glassy bowed-noise bands a semitone apart, uneven tremolo | ghost in sight and closing |
| Pulse | `ScorePulse`: low throb at 66 bpm, accent every fourth beat | ghost near, WARN, attacks |
| Swell | `ScoreSwell`: a rising cluster that cuts off | ghost closes in after ≥ 20 s of calm; once per 30 s at most |

One intensity value drives them, from the ghost's visibility, straight-line distance, behaviour and attack phase. It rises at 0.8/s and falls at 0.12/s; after a climax it cannot climb past "unease" for 9 s unless an attack starts; with nothing happening it withdraws almost completely for 14-24 s every 50-90 s. Near the top, the house ambience ducks 60 % and the storm is muffled further: the hush before an attack. Dead or escaped: everything fades.

**House events** (`AmbientEvents`): knocks inside the walls 4-9 m from the player, pipe groans at the kitchen and utility pipe runs, and a leak dripping by the kitchen sink (audible within about 7 m). One event every 9-22 s at most, none while the score is above 0.7, none unless the run is playing. Creaks and the storm stay with `StormController`.

All new sounds are synthesised in `ProceduralAudio` (nine cues: `GhostPresence`, `GhostDrag`, `ScoreDrone`, `ScoreStrings`, `ScorePulse`, `ScoreSwell`, `DistantKnock`×3, `PipeGroan`×2, `WaterDrip`×3) and mixed in `GameAudio`. One-shots use the existing pooled 3D sources, loops are owned by the scene, so nothing accumulates across restarts. There is no `AudioMixer` asset; levels stay in `GameAudio`'s mix table.

## Environmental storytelling

`BlackthornGreybox.Story.cs`, decoration only (no colliders, no shadows, nothing on the route waypoints):

- The caretaker's muddy footprints come in at the front door and stop in the middle of the hall.
- Wet grave soil is smeared from the locked cellar door up the corridor toward the kitchen, thinning as it goes.
- The cellar door is gouged from the corridor side at hand height.
- Crockery lies smashed by the kitchen table, beside the fallen chair.

## How presentation reads gameplay state

| Consumer | Reads | Never |
|---|---|---|
| `HollowVisual` | pose, speed, look target from `GhostMotionController` | knows about decisions or sensors |
| `GhostMotionController` | `GhostRuntime.CurrentState` (unchanged) | chooses a behaviour |
| `TensionScore`, `HorrorPostFx`, `PresenceLights` | ghost visibility, distance, `Behaviour`, `Phase`; `RunState` | write to the ghost, `WorldState` or telemetry |
| `AmbientEvents` | `RunState`, score intensity, player position | fabricate or touch physical events |

Gameplay-visible changes, deliberately small: idle (non-attack) turning toward the player is slower (110°/s), which affects only how quickly it *faces* you while following, protecting or watching.

## Cost and platform notes

The automated checks below ran on the Windows desktop build. The team has since demonstrated the game on Meta Quest 2 and Quest 3 as PC VR (26 Oct 2026, team-confirmed); see [`TechnicalSpec_MilestoneStatus.md`](TechnicalSpec_MilestoneStatus.md). Costs to keep an eye on in a headset:

- Robe cloth: about 5,100 vertices updated on the CPU per frame while the robe is on screen.
- Three extra shadowed spot lights (window light) in the additional-light shadow atlas.
- Lens distortion and chromatic aberration are uncomfortable in a headset and should be off there.
- Dust volumes are transparent overdraw (small: 225 particles total).

## Rebuilding the Ghost mesh

```bash
blender --background --python Tools/blender/build_hollow.py -- Assets/PHYSXR/Resources/PHYSXR/Hollow/Hollow.fbx
```

The script also has `assemble_preview()`, which lays the parts out in the same hierarchy `HollowVisual` builds, for judging the silhouette inside Blender.
